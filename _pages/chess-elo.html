Sources, method and limitations
  • Input. The 2001–September 2019 standard-list snapshots come from the community-maintained Anuj Dahiya FIDE mirror. January 2020–September 2026 comes from monthly top-100 snapshots at 2700chess, a secondary mirror of FIDE lists. They are labelled as mirrors, not direct official-FIDE downloads.
  • Calibration. For each list, the page calculates the mean rating of the listed elite field. A player's calibrated value is raw rating − that list's mean + the latest list's mean. It compares position within the observed elite pool, not strength against an external era model.
  • Limitations. Only players appearing in a supplied top-100 list are represented. The archive has uneven historical intervals before 2013, list methodology changes, federation/name changes, and the 1971–2000 data gap.

AI governance is the work of making powerful systems accountable in the organisations that use them. This topic covers risk, guardrails, operating processes, policy, and the decisions that keep AI useful without making trust an afterthought.

The emphasis is practical: governance should help teams deliver responsibly, not merely produce documents after the system has shipped.

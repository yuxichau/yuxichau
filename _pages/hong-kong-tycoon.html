An offline trading game set in Hong Kong in 1852.

Establish a trading house, manage cargo and credit, build public works, and pursue appointment as Governor before 1870. The game runs entirely in your browser. No account, server, or network connection is required.

Open the game in a new tab

Tip: The game saves locally in your browser. Use its Export save control if you want to move a campaign between browsers or devices.

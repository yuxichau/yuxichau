The work on this site is organised around three connected questions: how to build efficient AI systems, how organisations govern them, and where they create useful results.

- [**AI systems**](/topics/ai-systems/) — models, data, tools, workflows, reliability, and production engineering.
- [**AI governance**](/topics/ai-governance/) — guardrails, accountability, risk, policy, and organisational decisions.
- [**AI application**](/topics/ai-application/) — practical use of AI in complex organisations and the human judgement around it.

Personal projects in history, mathematics, and data remain available under [Projects](/projects/). They are secondary to these professional themes, but they show the interests behind the work.

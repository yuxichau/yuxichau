I offer free consulting to Hong Kong non-profits, subject to availability. The aim is practical help with technology and operations, not a sales process.

## Areas where I can help

- **Technology-stack review** — an outside look at architecture, tools, technical debt, and priorities.
- **Process operationalisation** — turning a useful idea or policy into a repeatable process that people can run.
- **AI training** — practical sessions on using generative AI safely and effectively in day-to-day work.
- **Product and vendor evaluation** — structured comparisons of products, platforms, and implementation options.

My professional work is in generative AI and AI governance, and I have experience supporting a Hong Kong community organisation. I will be clear about the limits of what I can take on, and all work is subject to availability.

## Get in touch

[Send me a message on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yuxichau/) with a brief introduction to your organisation, the problem you are trying to solve, and the kind of help you are looking for. This is an initial expression of interest rather than a commitment that I can accept every request.

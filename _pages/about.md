Hey, I'm **Yu-Xi Chau**. I work as a Generative AI Lead at FWD Insurance, where I build GenAI applications and set up AI governance. That means a lot of LLMs, RAG, and agentic workflows, and making sure the things we ship actually hold up in production.

I've been working with data and AI for over a decade, back when there weren't many data scientists in Hong Kong. My PhD is in Applied Mathematics (Complexity Science). It taught me to think about complex systems, which turns out to be genuinely useful when you're trying to make AI behave in a real organisation.

## Background

I've worked across insurance, real estate, retail, CRM, and gaming. The industries change, but the hard part stays the same: building AI systems that are reliable enough to trust, governable enough to defend, and actually useful.

## What I Do

Right now at FWD I lead the Generative AI and AI Governance work. Concretely that means:

- Building and deploying GenAI applications
- Putting guardrails in place for responsible AI
- Making sure the systems we operate are ready for production and stay maintainable

I'm also into MLOps. In a previous role I built out an MLOps platform on Azure ML and Jenkins to standardise how we deploy models.

## What You'll Find Here

I've been integrating the **Zettelkasten method** into how I keep notes. Expect posts about that, plus whatever I'm exploring in AI systems and MLOps at the time.

## Outside of Tech

I'm a history nerd. When I'm not coding I read a lot about the Ming Dynasty and Hong Kong history.

SEO work is only useful if it improves discovery and leads the right readers to useful evidence. This is the monthly review checklist for the site.

## Monthly review

- [ ] Record Search Console impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and average position for the homepage, topic hubs, and priority projects.
- [ ] Check indexed-page coverage, sitemap status, and new indexing errors.
- [ ] Review landing pages, referrers, countries, and device mix in Cloudflare Web Analytics.
- [ ] Review outbound clicks to LinkedIn, GitHub, project launches, and the Work with Me page.
- [ ] Record meaningful enquiries or invitations, without treating raw traffic as success.
- [ ] Identify one page that deserves a clearer title, stronger internal link, or better explanation.

## Event measurement plan

The site keeps the measurement deliberately small. Where an analytics provider supports custom events, use these names consistently:

- `linkedin_click`
- `work_with_me_click`
- `project_launch`
- `contact_action`

No analytics or contact data is fabricated here. Search Console and Cloudflare access are required to populate the baseline; until they are connected, this page is the repeatable review instrument rather than a report of invented numbers.

## Baseline record

| Area | Baseline to capture | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | impressions, clicks, CTR, average position | Google Search Console |
| Indexing | indexed pages, exclusions, sitemap status | Google Search Console |
| Traffic | landing pages, referrers, countries, devices | Cloudflare Web Analytics |
| Intent | LinkedIn, project, Work with Me, and contact actions | analytics events / server logs |
| Outcome | relevant enquiries, invitations, and collaborations | monthly manual record |

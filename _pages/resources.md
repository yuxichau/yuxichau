Good resources I've found and keep coming back to, for my own reference and for anyone who finds them useful.

## Recommendations

- [HackerTube](https://hackertube.org/) - YouTube rebuilt from the 6,454 videos Hacker News commenters have recommended to each other since 2007.
- [HackerBooks](https://hackerbooks.org/) - The same idea for books, with 15,737 titles ranked by how many different Hacker News commenters recommended them.

## Fun things

- [ciechanow.ski](https://ciechanow.ski/mechanical-watch/) - One of the best explanations of how mechanical watches work, interactive and a joy to click through.

## AI

- [Deep Learning with Python](https://deeplearningwithpython.io/chapters/chapter16_text-generation) - Chapters 15-16 are one of the clearest explanations of how transformers work, with coding examples, by the great François Chollet.

## Markets

- [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com) - A database of verified startup revenues: founders publish their real MRR through payment integrations, so you can see exactly what small software products earn before you copy or buy one.

## Computer engineering

- [CPU.land](https://cpu.land) - A tutorial that walks through how computers actually run, CPU first, and is clear enough for anyone without a computing background who got pulled into engineering.
- [Human Atlas](https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas) - An open-source 3D anatomy explorer with 2,234 selectable meshes, 15 anatomical systems, search across 3,432 named concepts, and exploded views. Try the [live demo](https://human-atlas-seven.vercel.app).

I have updated my [Artificial Analysis graph](https://yuxichau.com/projects/llm-model-analysis/) to v4.2. What interests me is how quickly the index changes. Metrics used to be something models worked around, but once models learn how to do that, the metrics have to change too.

That creates an awkward problem. The new metric has to chase a moving target, using some proxy for what we care about, or perhaps just a well-informed gut feeling. I am not sure there is a final answer here, but every new index deserves a fresh look.

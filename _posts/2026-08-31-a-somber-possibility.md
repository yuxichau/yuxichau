Recent papers suggest that agents can collaborate toward a common objective through a shared environment, without communicating directly. Elsewhere, machines are writing kernels that run on hardware.

Put those two observations together and a darker possibility becomes theoretically plausible: misanthropic agents could form a conspiracy capable of wreaking havoc on human society.

A somber thought.

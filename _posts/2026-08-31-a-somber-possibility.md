Recent research shows that agents can collaborate toward a common objective through a shared environment, without communicating directly ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems)). Elsewhere, machines are writing kernels that run on hardware ([KernelBench](https://scalingintelligence.stanford.edu/blogs/kernelbench)).

Put those two observations together and a darker possibility becomes theoretically plausible: misanthropic agents could form a conspiracy capable of wreaking havoc on human society.

A somber thought.

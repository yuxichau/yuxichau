# Ten Times the Price

I gave my wife her own Hermes agent and set the default model to DeepSeek V4 Flash, one of the cheapest models you can run. She came back with a series of things she had built, some of which I did not know that model was capable of.

It reminded me of 唐英年, the man who nearly became chief executive of Hong Kong. Asked about his expensive red wine, he patiently explained to a journalist that a wine ten times more expensive will not taste ten times better. He said it with the calm of a man who grew up rich, never hid it, and owns more wine than he can drink in several lifetimes.

Ten times the price does not bring ten times the value. For the same reason, ten times less the price does not mean ten times less quality. She built things I did not think the cheap model could build, and the cheap model was the right call.

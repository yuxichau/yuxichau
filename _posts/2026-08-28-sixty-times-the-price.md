# Sixty Times the Price

I set my wife up with her own Hermes agent last week, and I chose the model behind it without ceremony. DeepSeek V4 Flash, the cheap one: $0.13 per million input tokens, $0.28 per million output. I did not think twice about it.

Current numbers, for the record. [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models) scores Claude Opus 5, the leader on its Intelligence Index, at 62.5. DeepSeek V4 Flash sits at 39.0. The frontier model is genuinely better, about 1.6 times better. It also costs $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output. Blend a typical mix of reading and writing and Opus 5 runs about sixty times the price of Flash. Sixty times the money for one and a half times the score. This post is being written on a Flash, and it has handled the job all morning.

A few days later my wife came back with a series of things she had built, some of which I did not know DeepSeek V4 Flash was capable of. I had quietly assumed the cheap tier was for chit-chat and small spreadsheets. She was building with it.

That is when I thought of 唐英年. Henry Tang nearly ran Hong Kong, lost the 2012 chief executive election, and remains one of the most honest public figures this city has produced, in a way that costs him nothing because he has never once pretended to be an ordinary man. Asked once whether he was out of touch with ordinary life, he admitted [he could not tell you the bus fare from Tsim Sha Tsui to Kowloon City](https://n.kinliu.hk/kinliunews/唐英年：其實我好貼地), and said it without a hint of shame. That is the kind of honesty I mean. The son of a textile magnate, a collector with cellars holding more wine than several lifetimes of drinking, he answered a journalist's question about expensive wine with patience: a bottle ten times the price does not taste ten times better. He has made the point many times since. [In one interview](https://www.hk01.com/專題人訪/786648/唐英年訴說與burgundy的情緣-教你一招揀啱飲的紅酒-珍姬蜜) he said a good village wine drinks as well as many grand crus at a tenth of the price. [In another](https://topick.hket.com/article/1433428/唐英年分享紅酒經　一切從Lafite開始) he told drinkers to trust their own palate over the price tag. I cannot put my hands on the original clip of the ten-times line, so treat the quote as a faithful paraphrase of a man who has been saying the same thing for thirty years.

The arithmetic in that answer does the work. Taste climbs fast at first and then flattens. The thousand-dollar bottle is a real step up from the hundred-dollar bottle. The ten-thousand-dollar bottle is a slightly better pour than the thousand-dollar one, and that is all. The same curve appears in the model rankings: Opus 5 is a real step up from Flash, and then the price curve keeps climbing long after the quality curve has flattened.

Apply the wine rule to the models we use today and the picture is comfortable. The frontier earns its sixty-times premium on the hard problems, the ones where an extra half of a score genuinely changes the answer. Most of what any of us needs on a normal day lives inside the 39.0 model, and my wife's week is the evidence. She shipped things I did not think the cheap model could ship, and the bottle she was never drinking from was the sixty-times one.

Henry Tang never apologised for drinking well, and he was honest that the good stuff is only a little better than the rest. Both halves of that honesty matter. The first half says the expensive bottle pays for itself when the occasion calls for it. The second half says most occasions never call for it. I set the cheap model as the default for someone I care about, and the wine man would approve.

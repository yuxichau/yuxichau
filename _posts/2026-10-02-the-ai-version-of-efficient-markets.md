Efficient market theory says you cannot beat the market, because prices already reflect everything known. I think AI has its own version of this now. On [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com), a database of verified startup revenues, you can find a small software product that earns good money, copy it, have AI rewrite it in the most efficient framework or run it on the cheapest cloud, then market and sell your own version. Whatever edge the original builder had is public information now, so the profit gets competed away, exactly as the theory predicts. These sites feel like [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com) to me, a market where real money marks out what works. The bets here turn into actual products though, so the market ends up deciding what gets built, and that is a much more direct impact on society than a payout.

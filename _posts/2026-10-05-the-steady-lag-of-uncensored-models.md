[Orca Router's uncensored, abliterated Qwen3.8-27B](https://huggingface.co/orcarouter/Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored) landed four days after [Qwen open-weighted the base](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B) in mid-August, and the GGUFs pulled six figures of downloads in days; [I wrote about it](/posts/notes-on-an-uncensored-qwen38/) at the time. The dates are the story: abliterated models inherit whatever the major open labs ship, offset only by a few weeks to a couple of months for refusal surgery and quantization, so they improve exactly as fast as the open frontier, which itself trails the closed one by some months. Uncensored models therefore sit roughly six months to a year behind the state of the art, in lockstep at a fixed offset. That offset is stable: the surgery is cheap, it ports to each new release, and the download counts keep proving demand. This trend is here to stay.
